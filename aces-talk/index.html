Closing

Implications for the Ecosystem

Platforms

Traditional ads may lose value while new services (“GEO-as-a-service”) emerge. MCP-like interfaces for AI shoppers could counteract position biases — unclear given our headless experiments, and platforms take on a new role as “seller”.

Sellers

Risk of being invisible to agents. Need for continuous monitoring — and potential for GEO with listings continuously tuned per AI buyer via automated, simulation-based optimization pipelines.

Consumers

AI agents will reduce search friction, but risk homogeneous choices.

Regulators

Concerns include market concentration and the need for standardized reporting of agent testing beyond traditional failure rates on processes.
